## Part-Time Parliament Note

For the Paxos paper, keep the design direction closer to retro-futurist lecture
notes than full retro-futurist magazine.

The paper should feel:
- editorial and authored
- readable first
- slightly strange and atmospheric
- structured like strong teaching material rather than a spectacle piece

Use magazine energy selectively:
- chapter openings
- transitions
- hero moments
- demo framing

Keep the body experience in lecture-note territory:
- clear hierarchy
- strong reading rhythm
- controlled annotation/callout use
- restrained motion and ornament

Avoid turning this paper into:
- a generic dark technical dashboard
- a loud neon vaporwave poster
- a cluttered interactive experience

The goal is a coherent paper-length experience where the landing page, reading
pages, and demos all feel like they belong to the same world.
