## Paxos Made Simple Note

This track should feel cleaner, sharper, and less patient than the main Paxos paper.

The paper should feel:
- direct and plainspoken
- willing to call confusing writing confusing
- lighter on atmosphere
- more interested in clarity than ceremony

Use the visual language to reinforce that:
- bold, simple composition
- strong headline treatment
- selective glitch or distortion accents
- clear contrast and fast hierarchy

Keep the reading experience disciplined:
- short intros
- minimal decorative framing
- fewer explanatory detours
- demos and diagrams should clarify, not entertain

Avoid turning this paper into:
- a soft editorial reading room
- a generic developer dashboard
- a noisy cyberpunk poster
- a sarcastic joke page

The goal is for this track to feel like someone finally rewrote Paxos in normal language and had the nerve to keep cutting.
