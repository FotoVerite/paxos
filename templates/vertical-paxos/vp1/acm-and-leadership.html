VP I - ACM and Leadership

In Vertical Paxos, leadership is assigned by the ACM. For reliability it would make sense for the service to be its own paxos system but that is not strictly true nor needed for the proof.

Leadership is given by the master service sending a message with a matching ballot to potential leader node. If that ballot matches its next ballot it will assume leadership. Along with the message will be the parameter completeBal which is the last known ballot with all values settled.

The paper does not really spell out how old leaders learn they are done. In practice, they would eventually see it from the acceptors, most likely through a p2b response showing that the system has already moved on to a higher ballot
