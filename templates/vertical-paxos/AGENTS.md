## Vertical Paxos

### Tone
- Direct, technical, and slightly skeptical.
- Do not oversell the paper as elegant if the mechanism is awkward.
- Keep the writing plain. This track should sound like someone finally naming the real moving parts.
- A little snark is fine when the paper is being pompous, evasive, or needlessly formal.
- Keep the bite aimed at the paper or the mechanism, not at the reader.
- Use it sparingly. The point is to cut through dryness, not turn the whole section into a bit.

### Visual Direction
- Distinct from the other Paxos tracks, but still editorial.
- Systems atlas, not control room.
- Lighter, diagrammatic, and more architectural than ceremonial.
- Use axes, ruled columns, annotations, and layered planes over flashy effects.
- The page should feel like a technical schematic somebody can read, not an ops dashboard.

### What To Emphasize
- Vertical vs horizontal movement.
- Read and write quorums as separate structure.
- Configuration authority.
- Handoff between configurations.
- State transfer and continuity.
- The fact that membership change is not a side detail.
- The master as a distinct control surface, not background machinery.

### Avoid
- Generic “distributed systems platform” styling.
- Reusing PMMC or RSM surfaces without adapting them to Vertical Paxos.
- Repeating the darker industrial reconfiguration mood from RSM.
- Purple or green-first palettes already owned by other tracks.
- Decorative motion that does not teach anything.
