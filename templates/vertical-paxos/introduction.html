Introduction

For practical operations of a large paxos style system, reconfiguration that can happen while commands are in flight are a needed extension. To this end Lamport, Malkhi, and Zhou sketch out a new algorithm that uses a separate Configuration Master to control reconfiguration.

The paper is also concerned with showing how current primary-backup systems can be viewed as restricted instances of Vertical Paxos, and therefore can be reasoned about as having Paxos-derived safety.
