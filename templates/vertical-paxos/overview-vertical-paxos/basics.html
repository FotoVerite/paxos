Vertical Paxos Overview - Basics

Client

Usually the user-controlled way into the system. It sends an RPC to the replica side and waits for an answer.

Leader

The leader drives the round. Its job is to keep proposals moving and get the system to settle on one value instead of several conflicting ones.

Acceptors

Acceptors act as the filter on proposals and enforce the rules that keep the whole system safe.

Learner / Replica

This process what turns won commands into state, and sends the result back to the client.

Vertical Paxos algorithm is virtually the same as ones proposed in Paxos Made Simple except in two very important ways.
