Vertical Paxos Overview - Variations

In Vertical Paxos I, a new configuration becomes active immediately and begins accepting commands, while the previous configuration is still needed to supply any missing state. If multiple leaders fail before completing this transfer, a chain of configurations can accumulate.

Vertical Paxos II delays activation until the transfer is complete, ensuring the system only depends on the current and immediately previous configuration.

VP I is engineered for availability, VP 2 for clean reasoning where configuration bottlenecks are not a major concern.
