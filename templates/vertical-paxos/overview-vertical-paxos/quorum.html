Vertical Paxos Overview - Quorum

In earlier implementations of Paxos, distinctions between quorums for read and write were not emphasized, and the protocol did not treat reads as a separate class of operation.

In Vertical Paxos, the authors make this distinction explicit and explore how separating read and write quorums affects system behavior while still maintaining safety guarantees.
