Primary Backup - Optimization

The authors are making the direct case that Vertical Paxos is a mathematical algorithm that explains the safety of Primary Backups.

The linearizability of the system. Paxos. The ability of leasing for fast read commands, direct consequence of paxos. The ease of reconfiguration in a 2 acceptor system. That's also paxos. It is there to widen the paper’s claim: not just that Vertical Paxos works, but that familiar primary-backup systems and their optimizations can be redescribed as Paxos

While I agree I also think this is more politically motivated then needed for the paper. It is less about helping the reader understand the mechanism than about extending Paxos’s conceptual territory and asserting ownership over designs that were often treated as something separate.
