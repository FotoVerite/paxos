Primary Backup - Quorum and Leadership

Reconfiguration in the systems the authors are considering is typically triggered by failures in long-running deployments. Because older configurations are more likely to contain failed processes. The authors have stated that uptime and liveliness are the main goals of the new algorithm; keeping the read quorum small makes it much more likely that a new leader can successfully retrieve the necessary state during handoff. Each additional node required in the read quorum increases the chance that the system cannot complete reconfiguration without waiting on failed or unreachable processes.

From an implementation perspective, larger read quorums also constrain how aggressively a system can replace nodes. Even if the protocol does not require carrying nodes forward into the new configuration, the system must ensure that enough of the old configuration remains reachable to satisfy the read quorum. In practice, this often means delaying decommissioning, maintaining overlap between configurations, or provisioning additional nodes to ensure the old quorum can still be contacted.

The tradeoff is that shrinking the read quorum shifts the burden onto writes. Since future leaders may read from any valid read quorum, the current write quorum must be large enough to intersect with all such possibilities.

This can be summarized in the simple formula.

w + r > N
w = write quorum r = read quorum N = total nodes

As long as w plus r are greater than the total nodes in the system this will ensure the required intersection property for safety

Given this formula, in the extreme case, where a single acceptor forms a read quorum, the write quorum becomes the entire set of acceptors. This pushes complexity and cost into committing new values, while simplifying recovery from old configurations. This ignores checkpointing, which could reduce dependence on older configurations but introduces its own cost and complexity.

The authors point out in the case of of primary replication that one of the main additional benefits is that the system can have the leader locally complete the state transfer without need of sending any information from other acceptors. Leading to reconfiguration speedup and higher success rate.
