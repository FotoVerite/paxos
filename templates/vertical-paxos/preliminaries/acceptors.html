Preliminaries - Acceptors

Each Acceptor is in charge of storing it vote per slot per ballot.

Acceptors reject messages with ballots less than the currently known largest ballot.

Stripped of the notation, an acceptor maintains two pieces of state: the highest ballot it has seen, and the values it has already voted for.

The rest of the formalism around vote[a][b], “choosable”, and “safe” values is there to make the proof work. In practice, the system only needs to track the latest accepted value and enforce monotonic ballots.
