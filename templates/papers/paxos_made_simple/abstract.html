Abstract

This paper presents the Paxos algorithm, a practical and elegant solution to the Byzantine Generals Problem. Paxos enables a group of computers to reliably agree on a single value, even when some computers fail or messages are delayed or lost.

The algorithm is presented in a way that emphasizes understanding over formalism, making it accessible to practitioners who need to implement consensus in distributed systems.
