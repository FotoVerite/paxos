The Problem

In a distributed system, we have multiple computers that need to agree on a value, but some can fail and messages can be delayed or lost.

Requirements

  1. Safety: Only one value is chosen
  2. Liveness: Eventually, a value will be chosen
  3. Fault Tolerance: Works even when processes fail
