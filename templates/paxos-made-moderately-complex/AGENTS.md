## Paxos Made Moderately Complex Note

This track should feel denser, more system-minded, and more honest about the mess than Paxos Made Simple.

The paper should feel:
- technical without becoming sterile
- structured and controlled
- more operational than atmospheric
- like it respects the reader enough to show the difficult parts

Use the visual language to support that:
- tighter rhythm
- firmer layout structure
- stronger sense of machinery and flow
- less flourish, more signal

Keep the body experience focused:
- explain the moving parts directly
- surface invariants, edge cases, and failure paths clearly
- let diagrams and demos do real explanatory work
- keep motion restrained and purposeful

Avoid turning this paper into:
- the same visual voice as Paxos Made Simple
- a glossy product page
- a lecture-note fantasy world
- a wall of identical technical cards

The goal is for this track to feel like the point where the comforting story runs out and the real protocol starts.
