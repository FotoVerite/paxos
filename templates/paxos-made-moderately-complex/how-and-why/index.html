How & Why Paxos Works

This section covers the core protocol. It's where you learn how replicas coordinate to agree on command ordering, and why the algorithm guarantees safety even when things go wrong.

The two pieces

Multi-Paxos has two main components working together:

  • Replicas: They receive client requests, propose commands for slots, and execute commands in order.
  • The Synod Protocol: Acceptors reach consensus on which command goes in each slot using ballots.

Replicas are the public-facing layer. Clients talk to replicas. The Synod protocol is the engine underneath—it's what actually guarantees agreement.
