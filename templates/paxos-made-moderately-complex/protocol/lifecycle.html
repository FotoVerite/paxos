Proposal Lifecycle Overview

A leader $\lambda_2$ has spawned a scout for some ballot b. This scout sends out (p1a, $\lambda$, b) to all known acceptors, $\alpha_1$, $\alpha_2$, $\alpha_3$. These acceptors proceed to adopt ballot b because their $\alpha$.ballot_num is < b.

These three acceptors then send back (p1b, $\alpha$, b, $\alpha$.accepted), of which only two are needed for a quorum. Once two of these messages are received without any (p1b, $\alpha$, b', $\alpha$.accepted), the scout sends (adopted, b, pvals). The leader then switches to the active phase by setting its $\lambda$.active to true.

Meanwhile, a client sends a command request RPC to two replicas $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$, which send (propose, s, c) to $\lambda_2$. The leader checks its $\lambda$.proposals and sees that there is none for this s. Because this maintains the C2 invariant, it can safely spawn a commander to handle this proposal.

The spawned commander then sends (p2a, $\lambda_2$, (b, s, c)) to $\alpha_1$, $\alpha_2$, $\alpha_3$.

These acceptors, since b == $\alpha$.ballot_num, will add (b, s, c) to their $\alpha$.accepted state, and send back (p2b, $\alpha$, (b, s, c)). When a quorum of these messages reach the commander, it will send (decision, (s, c)) to the replicas and complete the process.
