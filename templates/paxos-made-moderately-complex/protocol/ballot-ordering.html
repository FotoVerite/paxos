Ballot Ordering

Definition
In this description, we will have ballot numbers be lexicographically ordered pairs of an integer and its leader identifier (consequently, leader identifiers need to be totally ordered as well). This way, given a ballot number, it is trivial to see who the leader of the ballot is.
We will use one special ballot number ⊥ that is ordered before any normal ballot number, but does not correspond to any ballot.
RENESSE, P. 4 §2

A ballot number is represented as a tuple (counter, leader_id), ordered lexicographically.

Given this format it is trivial to map a ballot to a leader by the second part of the tuple.

The special ballot number ⊥ is a sentinel that is ordered before all real ballot numbers and represents the absence of any prior ballot. It is used to initialize state so that the first real ballot always compares as greater, avoiding special-case logic.
