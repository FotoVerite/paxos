Commander Invariants

Commander Uniqueness

$\mathbf{C1}$: For any $b$ and $s$, at most one commander is spawned;

Invariant C1 implies Invariant A4, because by C1 all acceptors that accept a pvalue for a particular ballot and slot number received the pvalue from the same commander

Command Consistency

$\mathbf{C2}$: Suppose that for each α among a majority of acceptors (b, s, c) ∈ α.accepted. If b' > b and a commander is spawned for (b' , s, c'), then c = c'

Similarly, Invariant C2 implies Invariant A5.

This invariant is needed to ensure A5 is respected. We will see through how commanders deal with responses from acceptors and the scout process how this is actually accomplished.
