Non Volatile Memory

All leaders need to store three variables in non volatile memory so they can recover from failure.

λ.ballot_num

a monotonically increasing ballot number, initially (0, λ);
RENESSE, P. 6 — §2 Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

λ or lambda referrer to to the leaders identifier. So for a leader with identifier two it's ballot numbers would go (0,2), (1,2), (2,2)...

λ.active

a boolean flag, initially false
RENESSE, P. 7 — §2 Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

In this context, λ.active is a flag on the leader that indicates whether the leader considers its current ballot active, meaning it can make progress with that ballot.

Initially false: The leader hasn’t started Phase 1 or hasn’t yet established itself as the leader for that ballot.

Becomes true: Once the leader successfully completes Phase 1 (scout phase), it has gathered promises from a majority of acceptors and can safely start spawning commanders for Phase 2.

λ.proposals

a map of slot numbers to proposed commands in the form of a set of (slot number, command) pairs, initially empty. At any time, there is at most one entry per slot number in the set.
RENESSE, P. 7 — §2 Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

These are the (slot, command) pairs this leader is proposing to acceptors. Commands here can be changed by the leader until a value is chosen, but there cannot be more than one command per slot.
