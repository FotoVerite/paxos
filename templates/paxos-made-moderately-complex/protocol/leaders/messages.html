Leader Messages

The leader starts by spawning a scout for its initial ballot number, and then enters into a loop awaiting messages
RENESSE, P. 7 §2 - Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

Leaders can receive three types of messages. One external, and two from internal processes.

Propose

(propose,s,c)
A replica proposes command c for slot number s
RENESSE, P. 7 §2 - Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

Replica's are send command's from clients with their current slot_number, ρ.slot_number

Adopted

(adopted, ballot num, pvals)
Sent by a scout, this message signifies that the current ballot number ballot num has been adopted by a majority of acceptors. (If an adopted message arrives for an old ballot number, it is ignored.) The set pvals contains all pvalues accepted by these acceptors prior to ballot_num.
RENESSE, P. 7 §2 - Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

Preempted

(preempted,(r', λ'))
Sent by either a scout or a commander, this message indicates that some acceptor has adopted $(r', \lambda')$. If $(r', \lambda')$ is greater than the leader’s current ballot number, it may no longer be possible to use that ballot to choose a command.
RENESSE, P. 7 §2 - Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

This internal message is an alert to the leader that it might no longer be in an active state. The leader checks whether its current ballot number is less than the one in the received message. r' here refers to the monotonically increasing round number in the tuple. If so, the leader selects a new ballot number $(r'', \lambda)$ with $r'' > r'$, sets itself to passive, and will retry later. Otherwise, it ignores the message.
