Preemption

Definition

If either a scout or a commander detects that an acceptor has adopted a ballot number b, with b > ballot num, then it sends the leader a preempted message. The leader becomes passive and spawns a new scout with a ballot number that is higher than b.

RENESSE, P. 9 §2 - Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

A leader can receive a (preempted, (r',λ')) from either a scout or a commander. It's mode does not matter because as soon as a greater ballot is in the system the current ballot will never win. When this happens the message is for the leader to increment it's state to (r'+1, λ). It then spawns a new scout to try and get this ballot adopted by $\mathcal{A}$.

Notice at the moment as the system is described that with multiple leaders, livelocking can happen and that leadership switching is inevitable
