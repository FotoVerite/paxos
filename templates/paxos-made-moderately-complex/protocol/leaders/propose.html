Propose

(propose,s,c)

If a new proposal arrives while the leader is active, the leader checks to see if it already has a proposal for the same slot (and has thus spawned a commander for that slot) in its set proposals. If not, the new proposal will satisfy Invariant C2, and thus the leader adds the proposal to proposals and spawns a commander.

RENESSE, P. 9 §2 - Scouts, Passive and Active Modes

When a leader receives a propose message from a replica, its action depends on its mode:

  • Passive: The proposal is ignored, since the leader is not currently active.
  • Active: The leader checks λ.proposals for the slot:
    • If the slot already has a command, the proposal is a duplicate and is ignored.
    • If the slot is not in λ.proposals, adding it satisfies Invariant C2. The leader adds the proposal to λ.proposals and spawns a commander to propose it to $\mathcal{A}$.
