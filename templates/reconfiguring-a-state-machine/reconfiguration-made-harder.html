Reconfiguration Made Harder

Why It Gets Hard

Once assumptions are relaxed, old and new configurations can overlap in time. Delays and failures make simple handoff rules insufficient.

Operational Risk

Without strict transition semantics, systems can oscillate between views or accept stale leadership, causing churn and potential liveness collapse.

Implementation Direction

Introduce explicit transition phases and guard rails (epoch checks, activation gates, catch-up requirements) before allowing newly configured leaders to propose.
